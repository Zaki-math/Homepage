Research

I am a mathematical biologist working at the intersection of applied dynamical systems, fractional calculus, and optimal control theory. My research focuses on developing data-informed models for infectious disease transmission, integrating stability analysis, sensitivity analysis, and AI-based frameworks (PINNs, deep learning) to design optimal intervention strategies. I explore problems in virology, host–pathogen interactions, and ecological epidemiology, with particular emphasis on emerging infections, vaccination policy, and evolutionary dynamics of resistance.
